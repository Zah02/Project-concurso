ABOUT THE GAME🌍 Biodiversity Guardians Biodiversity Guardians is a 2D top-down action game about protecting nature, saving endangered animals, and fighting the pollution that threatens our planet. Explore different biomes, defeat powerful guardians, and restore balance to a world corrupted by pollution. Every victory brings nature one step closer to recovery. 📖 The Lore Once upon a time, nature lived in perfect balance. Oceans were clean, forests were full of life, and animals could live safely in their natural habitats. But as pollution spread across the planet, everything began to change. The oceans became toxic, wildfires destroyed forests, deserts grew more dangerous, and ice-covered lands started to lose their balance. The pollution eventually gained a life of its own, corrupting powerful creatures and turning them into dangerous guardians. These guardians now protect the corrupted regions, preventing nature from recovering. You play as a small hero with a special mission: defeat the corrupted guardians, free the endangered animals, and face the source of all pollution — the Final Boss. The planet's future depends on you. 🗺️ The Biomes🌊 Sea The ocean has been damaged by water pollution and waste. Its guardian represents the destruction of marine ecosystems. Defeating it frees a sea turtle, giving marine life a chance to return. 🏜️ Drylands Rising temperatures, drought, and fires have damaged this harsh environment. Its guardian represents the destructive effects of environmental damage. Defeating it frees a desert fox. 🌲 Forest Once full of life, the forest has been threatened by pollution and destruction. Its guardian represents the damage caused to natural habitats. Defeating it frees a lynx. ❄️ Snow The frozen lands are suffering from environmental changes and the loss of their natural balance. Its guardian represents the dangers threatening cold ecosystems. Defeating it frees an Arctic fox. ☠️ The Final Boss The Final Boss is the ultimate source of the world's corruption. It represents the combined threat of pollution and environmental destruction. You must defeat the four biome guardians before facing this final enemy. Only then can you complete your mission and restore balance to the planet. 🐾 Endangered Animals Every guardian protects a corrupted biome. When a guardian is defeated, the endangered animal associated with that environment is freed. The rescued animals briefly appear before escaping toward the edge of the screen, symbolizing their return to freedom. Sea Turtle — represents marine wildlife threatened by ocean pollution. Desert Fox — represents wildlife living in fragile, dry environments. Lynx — represents the importance of protecting forests and their biodiversity. Arctic Fox — represents wildlife threatened by changes in cold environments. Each rescue is a small victory for nature. ⚔️ Gameplay Biodiversity Guardians combines exploration, fast-paced combat, and boss battles in a simple pixel-art world. Key features: Explore a world divided into different biomes. Fight corrupted guardians and the Final Boss. Use automatic attacks to target enemies. Manage ammunition and reload at the right moment. Dodge enemy projectiles and protect your health. Free endangered animals by defeating guardians. Unlock the final confrontation by defeating the biome guardians. 🎮 Controls W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys — Move the character. Mouse Click / Hold — Shoot. Spacebar — Shoot. Back Button — Return to the previous page. 🌱 The Message Behind the Game Biodiversity is essential for life on Earth. Every species plays a role in its ecosystem, and the loss of even one species can affect many others. Pollution, habitat destruction, wildfires, and climate change threaten ecosystems around the world. Although this game presents these problems through fictional enemies and fantasy environments, its message is real. Protecting nature means protecting the future of every living species, including our own. 🏆 Your Mission Defeat the guardians. Rescue the animals. Stop the corruption. Restore nature. Protect biodiversity. Save the planet. The future of this world is in your hands.
