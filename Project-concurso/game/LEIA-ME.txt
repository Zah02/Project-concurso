GUARDIANS OF BIODIVERSITY — PROTOTYPE

HOW TO RUN
1. Keep all folders and files in the same structure.
2. Open the folder in VS Code.
3. Run index.html with Live Server (recommended).

CONTROLS
- WASD or arrow keys: move
- Hold SPACE or hold the left mouse button on the game: continuous shooting
- ESC: pause
- Fullscreen: toggle full-screen layout
- Restart: restart the adventure

FEATURES
- Centered game camera with status panel, guardian silhouettes, and world minimap on the right.
- Guardian silhouettes become marked as defeated when each boss is beaten.
- Boss attacks use a 12-shot magazine, 0.2-second firing interval, and 2-second reload.
- Defeat the final boss, then walk over the trophy to trigger the You Win screen.

ART FILES
- mapa/mundo.png: original 1024x512 world map
- sprites/jogador-parado.png: idle player sprite
- sprites/jogador-andar.png: walking player sprite
- sprites/inimigo-mar.png: sea guardian sprite
- sprites/inimigo-seco.png: drylands/forest guardian placeholder sprite
- sprites/boss-final.png: final boss sprite

NOTES

- Boss positions and biome boundaries are approximate and can be adjusted.
