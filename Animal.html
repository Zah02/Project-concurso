Hawksbill Sea Turtle: Critically Endangered

The hawksbill sea turtle is threatened with extinction due to ocean pollution, plastic waste and abandoned fishing nets, which can injure or kill it. The destruction of coral reefs and illegal hunting also put its survival at risk. Protecting our oceans is essential to ensure this species survives for future generations.

North Atlantic Right Whale: Critically Endangered

The North Atlantic right whale is at risk of extinction due to human activities. Noise pollution from ships can interfere with its communication and ability to find food. It is also threatened by entanglement in fishing nets and collisions with ships, which can cause serious injuries or death. Protecting these whales and reducing ocean pollution are essential to ensure their survival.

Hector’s Dolphin and Other Cetaceans: Threatened by Pollution

Hector’s dolphins and other cetaceans are threatened by plastic waste and chemical pollutants in the oceans. These harmful substances can be ingested or enter their bodies through the food chain, causing illness and affecting their reproduction. Protecting marine habitats and reducing ocean pollution are essential to ensure their survival.

Tropical Frogs: Threatened by Pollution

Many tropical frog species are vulnerable to pesticides, chemical waste and water pollution. These harmful substances can damage their sensitive skin, affect their reproduction and threaten their survival. Protecting clean water sources and preserving tropical forests are essential to keeping these frogs safe.

Axolotl: Critically Endangered

The axolotl is a unique amphibian that is critically endangered due to water pollution and habitat loss. Contaminated water can harm its health, while urban development and the destruction of freshwater habitats threaten its survival. Protecting clean water and restoring its natural habitat are essential to help this remarkable species survive.

Albatrosses: Several Species Endangered

Many albatross species are threatened by human activities and ocean pollution. They often swallow plastic waste because they mistake it for food, which can cause serious injuries or death. They can also become trapped in fishing equipment, making it difficult to fly or survive. Reducing plastic pollution and using safer fishing methods are essential to protect these magnificent seabirds.
